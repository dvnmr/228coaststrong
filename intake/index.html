Coast Strong — Bayou Watch Hub is a community page for the Mississippi Gulf Coast. Agency and organization contacts are copied from official public pages researched Oct 15, 2026. This page does not submit or store reports, and nothing is sent in your name from this page. Participation and sponsor inquiries use the email links above. Review and send through your own email app; this page has no signup database. For events run by official programs, verify details on their own official pages.

A small note on how this started: Coast Strong began with one Biloxi neighbor, Devin Moore, who found that dumped TV in the marsh at Bayou Bernard Park on October 15, 2026, and hauled it out. He started this for his family — for Hazel Moore, for Albert Rhett Moore, for Jamie Jarvis Gilbert, for Michelle Gilbert, for Brett Moore, and for Sharon Moore — and that is the whole story of him on this page. This was never about him. It's about the community, and it's about fixing things.
